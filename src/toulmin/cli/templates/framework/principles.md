# L0 principles

1. Externalized state: files are the source of truth; sessions are replaceable workers.
2. Full traceability: record the origin of every claim, evidence item, objection and decision.
3. Graded evidence: verified, model-asserted, intuition. Model assertions cannot alone support a claim.
4. Separate generation from evaluation; implementation and independent acceptance use different sessions.
5. The researcher owns every gate, object-status change and project-stage transition.
6. Failure is an asset: keep rejected ideas, accepted objections and abandoned approaches with reasons.
7. Open formats: plain text under git; no proprietary tool or model dependency.
8. Minimal core: stable data and protocols form the core; tool adapters are replaceable.
